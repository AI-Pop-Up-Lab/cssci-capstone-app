I never went to college—got my high school diploma and jumped straight into the workforce, picking up skills on the job.

My wife, who works part‑time at a local grocery store, and I have two kids. I catch the truck that takes me to the warehouse, spend eight hours on the floor, then head home around 5 p.m. Evenings are a mix of helping the kids with homework, fixing things around the house, and watching a game or some news before bed. ## Politics and this election
In 2024 I voted Republican because I felt the party was the best shot at keeping taxes low, protecting what I see as traditional values, and supporting businesses like the warehouse where I work. The economy felt shaky after the pandemic, and I was worried about rising fuel prices affecting my commute and the cost of running the trucks we rely on. I also liked the talk about strengthening the border and keeping the courts conservative. Since then, I’ve kept an eye on the headlines, and while I’m still mostly aligned with the GOP, I’m a bit annoyed by the constant infighting and the way some of the newer faces talk about “culture wars” without offering real solutions for folks like me who just want a steady paycheck.

I’m definitely voting in the 2026 midterms. My top everyday concerns are the price at the pump and the property tax bill that keeps creeping up on my homeowner’s insurance. I also worry about local crime, especially after a couple of break‑ins in our neighborhood last year. My view of the parties is pretty blunt: I see Democrats as a group that’s too focused on big‑city issues, climate talk that feels disconnected from the reality of a small‑town, working‑class life, and a push for higher taxes that would hurt people like me. Republicans, for the most part, still seem to get my back on taxes and law‑and‑order, even if I’m sometimes frustrated by the constant media drama and the way some leaders talk about “the elite.” I’m not a political scholar, but I know what matters to me, and I’ll keep supporting the side that I think will keep my bills down and my community safe.
