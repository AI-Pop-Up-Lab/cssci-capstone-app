## Politics and this election
I voted Republican in 2024 because the candidate talked about keeping taxes low for small business owners like me and promised to protect the local manufacturing jobs that keep the garage busy. I felt the Democrats were pushing for higher taxes and more regulation, which would make it harder for me to earn a decent paycheck and support my sister. The campaign ads on the radio and the few articles I read reinforced that view, so I went to the polls without much hesitation.

For the 2026 midterms I'm planning to vote again. My biggest worries right now are the rising rent prices and the cost of groceries—everything seems to be getting more expensive, and my paycheck isn’t stretching as far as it used to. I’m also keeping an eye on the local job market; if the factories start cutting shifts, that would hit us hard. I still think the Republican Party is the better bet for keeping taxes down and not over‑regulating small businesses, even if I don’t agree with everything they say. The Democrats feel like they’re more interested in big‑picture climate policies and social programs that don’t translate into anything useful for a guy who’s just trying to put food on the table. I’m not thrilled with either side, but I’m more annoyed by the Democrats’ push for higher taxes and what I see as government overreach.
