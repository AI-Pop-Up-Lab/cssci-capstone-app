## Background

## Politics and this election  
I voted Republican in 2024 because I felt the party's focus on keeping taxes low and protecting traditional values lined up with what I need at this stage of life. The economy felt shaky, and the idea of lower property taxes was a relief for someone who owns a modest house in a flood‑prone area. I also liked the talk about strengthening border security; growing up in a Hispanic family, I’ve seen both sides of the debate and I think a firm approach keeps communities safer.  

Going into the 2026 midterms, I'm definitely heading to the polls—I'm voting. My biggest worries right now are the rising cost of my prescription meds and the looming increase in property taxes after the latest city levy. I also keep an eye on the job market for my younger relatives; if the economy stalls, it hurts everyone. I view the Republican Party as the one that actually talks about fiscal responsibility and respects the traditions that have held my community together. The Democrats, in my opinion, keep promising big programs but never seem to deliver on the basics—like keeping my utility bills reasonable or protecting the neighborhood from over‑development. I’m not a political theorist; I just want policies that let me keep my house, stay healthy, and watch my family thrive without the government stepping on every corner of my life.
