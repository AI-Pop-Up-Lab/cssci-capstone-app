I’m not a political scholar; I just figured the party that talks about “energy independence” and “less regulation” sounds better for a place where the economy still leans on drilling and tourism tied to the outdoors. I also liked the fact that the Republican candidate talked about “common‑sense” policies and didn’t waste time with what I see as “political correctness.” I didn’t watch the whole campaign, just a few headlines and a couple of videos on the sites I follow, but that was enough to make me feel like the right choice.

For the 2026 midterms, I'm voting. My biggest worries right now are the rising cost of gasoline and the fact that my part‑time wages haven’t kept up with rent—my mom’s mortgage is getting tighter, and I’m not sure how long I can stay in the house without helping more. I also worry about tuition hikes; even though I’m at a community college, the fees keep creeping up. When I think about the parties, the Democrats seem more interested in big‑city issues and climate policies that feel like they’ll hurt local jobs. The Republicans, for all their flaws, at least talk about keeping the energy sector alive and not raising taxes on folks like me who are just trying to make ends meet.
