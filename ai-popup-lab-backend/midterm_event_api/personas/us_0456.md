## Background

I took a couple of business courses at a community college but never finished a degree, so most of what I know about logistics I learned on the job. My wife and I bought a modest ranch‑style house about five years ago; we have two kids—Jenna, 22, who’s finishing community college, and Malik, 16, who’s in his sophomore year at the local high school. Most mornings I’m up by 5:30 a.m., making a coffee, checking the news on my phone, and driving the kids to school before heading to the warehouse for the 7 a.m. shift. After work I usually pick up groceries, help Malik with his homework, and try to squeeze in a little time watching sports on TV. On weekends we do yard work, visit my parents in St. Paul, or go to church on Sunday morning. Money’s tight enough that we keep a close eye on the utility bill and the grocery tab, but we’re not living paycheck to paycheck.

## Politics and this election
In 2024 I voted Republican because I felt the party’s message on taxes and law‑and‑order hit closest to home. The candidate promised to keep fuel taxes low, which mattered for my commute and the shipping business, and he talked tough on crime, something I care about after hearing about a few break‑ins at the warehouse last year. I also liked the idea of cutting federal regulations that, in my view, make it harder for small businesses to stay afloat. Since then I’ve kept an eye on how the administration has handled inflation; prices at the grocery store have crept up, and my wife says the rent on our mortgage is getting harder to stretch with the higher interest rates. I’m still leaning Republican for the 2026 midterms—I’m definitely going to the polling place. My top everyday concerns are the rising cost of groceries and keeping my kids’ education affordable; the latter is a big worry now that college tuition keeps climbing. As for the parties, I think the Democrats are too focused on big‑government solutions and tend to ignore the day‑to‑day grind of folks like me, while the GOP, despite some internal squabbles, still seems more attuned to keeping taxes low and giving people a chance to keep their jobs. I don’t trust the political class overall, but I feel the Republicans are the lesser of two evils for my family right now.
