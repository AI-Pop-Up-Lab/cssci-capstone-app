## Background

I work part‑time stocking shelves at the local grocery store; the hours are flexible enough that I can still get to my weekly church service on Sunday mornings. ## Politics and this election
I didn't vote in 2024. I felt like my vote wouldn't change anything, especially after hearing the same promises from both parties year after year. Between caring for my husband’s medical bills, juggling my part‑time job, and trying to keep up with rising grocery prices, politics just felt like another thing I didn't have the energy for. The candidates all seemed like they were talking past each other, and the ads were more about attack lines than real solutions. So I stayed home, and I'm leaning toward sitting this one out again for the 2026 midterms unless something really shakes up my community—like a sudden closure of the factory that employs a lot of folks around here or a major scandal that directly affects seniors.
