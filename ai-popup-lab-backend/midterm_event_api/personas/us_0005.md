## Politics and this election

Looking ahead to the 2026 midterms, you’re leaning toward voting if a candidate can show concrete plans for expanding tele‑health services and protecting funding for rural schools. Your top concerns are affordable healthcare for your family and reliable, affordable energy. You view the Democratic Party as more likely to push for social safety nets, while the Republicans seem focused on resource extraction; both, however, feel distant from everyday life in interior Alaska.
