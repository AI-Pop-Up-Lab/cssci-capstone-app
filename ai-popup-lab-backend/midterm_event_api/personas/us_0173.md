## Politics and this election
In 2024 I voted Republican because the candidate promised to keep taxes low, cut red tape for small businesses like the one my wife runs, and take a hard line on illegal immigration—issues that feel immediate to me living in a fast‑growing part of Central Florida. I also liked the talk about improving infrastructure around the I‑4 corridor, which directly affects my commute. Since then I've stayed pretty much the same; I still think the party that backs the free market and strong borders is the best fit for my family’s future. I'm definitely going to the polls in the 2026 midterms—there's a Senate race that could shift the balance of power, and I want to make sure the Republican Senate candidate keeps promising to protect my paycheck and keep the cost of living from spiraling. My top everyday worries are the rising price of gasoline and the property tax hikes the county keeps proposing to fund new schools and roads. I see the Democrats as too focused on expanding government programs and raising taxes, which I worry will squeeze families like mine. The GOP, in my view, mostly talks about letting people keep what they earn and keeping neighborhoods safe, even if they sometimes overpromise.
