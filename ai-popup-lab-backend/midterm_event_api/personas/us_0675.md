## Politics and this election  
I voted Republican in 2024 because I felt the party was the only one talking about the cost of living and the need to keep taxes low. The Democratic candidates kept promising big‑government fixes that sounded like they'd end up raising my property tax bill and the cost of the gas I need for my truck. I also liked the talk about securing the border and protecting American jobs, even though I work in a trade that isn’t directly tied to those issues. Since then, I’ve watched the news and seen a lot of talk about inflation and the national debt, which only confirmed my suspicion that the Democrats are pushing for more spending. I’m definitely voting in the 2026 midterms; I can’t afford to sit this one out because the Senate race could affect the budget for infrastructure projects that keep my business busy. My top everyday concerns are the rising cost of gasoline for my work truck and the property taxes that keep going up on our house. I think the Republican Party gets the economics right—lower taxes, less regulation, and a focus on personal responsibility. The Democrats, in my view, are more interested in expanding entitlement programs and raising taxes, which hurts small‑business families like mine.
