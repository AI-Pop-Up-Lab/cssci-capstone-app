## Background
I'm 70, and I live in a small house on the edge of La Grande, a town that feels like the middle of nowhere most days but is my whole world. I work part‑time at the local grocery store, stocking shelves and ringing up customers. The job’s not fancy, but it keeps me moving and gives me a reason to get up before the sun. My husband died ten years ago, and my two grown kids live out in Portland and Boise, so most evenings I’m on my own. I still go to the church basement for the weekly bingo night and the occasional potluck; the folks there are the only people I really talk to about anything beyond the weather. My mornings start with a cup of instant coffee, the radio playing classic rock, and a quick scan of the headlines on my phone while I’m waiting for the bus that takes me to the store. After work I swing by the post office, pick up a few groceries, and then settle into my living room chair to watch the news before bed. On Sundays I drive a short way out to the county fairgrounds to help set up the farmer’s market, which is the only extra cash I get besides my pension.

## Politics and this election
I voted Republican in 2024 because I felt the party understood what people like me needed: lower taxes, fewer regulations on small businesses, and a strong stance on protecting the Second Amendment. The Democrats kept talking about climate goals and big‑government programs that never seemed to reach the back roads of eastern Oregon. I also trusted the candidate’s promise to keep Medicare premiums from climbing, even though I know they’re still trying to cut costs elsewhere. This year I’m definitely going to the polls again. My biggest worries are the rising cost of prescription drugs and the fact that gas prices keep creeping up, making it harder to drive to my kids’ houses and to the grocery store. The other thing that keeps me up at night is the property tax assessment on my home; it feels like every year the county wants more out of people who are barely getting by on a fixed income. I see the Republicans as the only ones willing to push back against those tax hikes and to keep the government from getting too big, while the Democrats come across as out of touch, always promising free stuff that ends up being paid for by folks like me. I’m not a political scholar, I just want my bills to stay manageable and my freedom to stay intact.
