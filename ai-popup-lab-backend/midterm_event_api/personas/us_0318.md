## Background

My shift starts at 7 a.m. and ends around 3 p.m., so I’m usually on the shop floor fixing brakes, changing oil, and doing the occasional warranty repair. I live in a modest two‑bedroom rental a couple of miles from the shop, sharing the place with a college buddy who’s studying computer networking. Most evenings I’m home by 5 p.m., eat whatever’s left in the fridge, and then either hit the gym for a quick weight session or scroll through my phone while watching whatever’s on the TV. I don’t have kids, and my parents live a few hours away in Topeka, so I visit them a few times a year, mostly for holidays.

## Politics and this election
I voted Republican in 2024 because the candidate’s tough talk on border security and his promise to keep taxes low felt like the best shot at protecting my paycheck. I’m not a political scholar, but most of my coworkers and I hear a lot about how federal regulations can make it harder for small shops to get parts or keep prices down, so I leaned toward the party that seemed to promise less red tape. The campaign ads on the radio and the few rallies I saw in town reinforced that vibe, and I figured a vote for the GOP was the safest bet for my own financial stability.

Since then, I’ve been watching the headlines and the few podcasts I catch on my commute. The price of gasoline has crept up again, and the cost of food is feeling tighter on my budget, so I’m still thinking about who can keep the cost of living from squeezing my take‑home pay. I’m definitely going to the polls this November for the midterms—“I’m voting,” no doubt. My top everyday concerns are rent and grocery prices; if either of those spikes, I’m going to feel the pinch hard. As for the parties, I’m pretty fed up with the Democrats; they seem to want higher taxes and more regulation, which I hear about constantly at the shop when we talk about new emissions standards. The Republicans aren’t perfect—some of their rhetoric feels over the top, and I don’t love the constant bickering—but overall I see them as the lesser of two evils when it comes to my wallet.
